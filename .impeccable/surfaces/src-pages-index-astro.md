# Surface brief: walkerproof.com (all pages)

Revision 3. Direction pinned by the owner: copy the DesignLab Framer template (designlab.framer.website) for structure, colours, width and motion. Code-led build. This replaces the earlier cream/red briefs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gear-care guide site dressed as a modern product landing page: black stage, light-weight white Inter, lime actions, one orange glow. It refuses the blog look.
OWN-WORLD: Black #000 ground, white type at 300 weight, lime #DDFF00 pills with black text, orange radial glow, glass cards with hairline borders and 28px corners, floating pill nav with an active state, light theme behind a storage-free toggle.
STORY: A visitor sees a clear promise (waterproof gear fails quietly, here is how to test, fix and choose it), trusts it because nothing tracks them, then reads a guide, takes the checklist or subscribes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating pill nav; left a light 5.5rem headline "Waterproof, until it isn't.", lead text, lime "Read the guides" button; right a glass gear-check board over an orange glow; marquee of covered topics below.
FORM: Sections with ids: hero, covered (marquee), how (sticky 3 steps), why (4 cards), topics (masonry with sticky heading and parallax), labels (explainer), guides, offer (checklist plus newsletter), faq, footer.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
