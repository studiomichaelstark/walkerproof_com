# Surface brief: walkerproof.com (all pages)

Mode: Persuade on home, checklist, newsletter pages; Read on guides. Direction pinned by the owner's brief (no concept roll). Build path: code-led. Revision 2: owner asked for less colour, a centred composition and red as accent only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gear-care publication set like outdoor sportswear branding in a restrained palette: giant uppercase Inter on a cream page, centred, with one red accent and typographic lists instead of colour blocks. It refuses the colour-field landing and the card-grid default.
OWN-WORLD: Cream #F2E9D8 body background, warm dark ink text, red #A60321 only as accent (primary button, one highlighted word, hover), sand #D9A577 in a single band, one ink band for contrast. No brown, no coral. Dark theme: neutral near-black ground, sand replaces red for accent text. Outlined pill buttons, hairline rules, tone-on-tone photo placeholders with the wobble mark, 800-weight uppercase display.
STORY: A visitor understands in one viewport: practical, honest gear-care guides. They believe it because nothing tracks them. They read a guide, take the checklist, or subscribe.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with centred wordmark and centred nav row. Centred display headline "GEAR THAT LASTS." with "LASTS." in the accent, one sentence, two pill buttons (red solid, ink outline), then a wide photo placeholder panel.
FORM: Centred editorial landing, seven id'd sections: hero, topics as a typographic list with guide counts, latest guides grid, ink split on privacy, sand checklist band, newsletter, footer with huge wordmark.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
