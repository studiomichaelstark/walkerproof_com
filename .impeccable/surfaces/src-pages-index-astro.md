# Surface brief: walkerproof.com (all pages)

Mode: Persuade on home, checklist, newsletter pages; Read on guides. Direction is pinned by the owner's brief, so no concept roll was run. Build path: code-led (no image generation, no photos yet).

Audience/job: broad outdoor community; read guides, subscribe, request checklist. Constraints: Inter only, no animation, no storage, section ids, palette fixed, brown rare.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gear-care publication that looks like outdoor sportswear branding: giant uppercase Inter, a red field that owns the page, one huge wordmark. It refuses the cream-blog-with-serif default and the card-grid default.
OWN-WORLD: Committed color strategy. Red #A60321 as full-bleed fields, cream #F2E9D8 as paper and as text on red, coral and sand as secondary full fields, dark ink for text on them, brown nearly absent. Dark theme swaps the ground to a warm near-black and lifts accents to coral. Outlined pill buttons, hairline rules, square-cornered photo placeholders with the wobble mark tone-on-tone, 800-weight uppercase display.
STORY: A visitor understands in one viewport: practical, honest gear-care guides. They believe it because nothing tracks them. They read a guide, take the checklist, or subscribe.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red field. Left: display headline "GEAR THAT LASTS." at about 6rem on desktop, one sentence below, two pill buttons (Read the guides, Get the checklist). Right (desktop) / below (mobile): oversized wobble badge cropped by the field edge. Header above: small wordmark, nav, theme toggle.
FORM: Editorial sportswear landing, seven id'd sections: hero, wordmark band, topics row, guides row, private-by-design split, checklist split, newsletter field, footer with huge wordmark. Not from a roll; pinned by the owner's HYLO reference.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
